1Who this is for

The person, the moment, and what they are doing instead today.

Solo founders and two-person teams who bill through Stripe and want to know, without a spreadsheet, whether the last month was better or worse than the one before. They currently open the Stripe dashboard, screenshot the MRR chart, and paste it into a notes app. That works until the third month, when the screenshots stop being comparable.

Long paragraphs are the real test of a body font. This one has a few words worth reading twice: state.json is written only by the server; the exporter turns data-radio blocks into Markdown with the chosen option marked. If you can skim this without your eye snagging, the font and line height are doing their job.

"I don't want a dashboard. I want to know if I should be worried." — interview 4, indie SaaS founder, $3.1k MRR
In plain words: the product answers one question a month, and it has to be right.
Risk: Stripe's API rate limits make an hourly refresh across 10k users expensive. The worker must batch by account, not by user.

2Stack decisions

Each block is one choice. Decisions save instantly; comments queue until you confirm.

D1 — How often do we pull from Stripe?
Hourly matches TrustMRR. Daily is 24× cheaper and nobody checks MRR at 3am.
D2 — Which payment processors at launch?
Every extra processor is a second adapter to keep passing CI.

→ 429 Retry-After: 30   (per-account rate limit)
